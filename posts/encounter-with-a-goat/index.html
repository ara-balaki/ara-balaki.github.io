Encounter with a goat

November 12, 2026

Once, when I was a mere child, I was on my merry way when I joyfully entered the alleyways next to my childhood home. Narrow corridors with walls formed from stone and concrete, patches of overgrown weeds, tiled with pebbles, leading out into an open field where I usually played among the tall grass and big leaves.

I turned left. I turned right. Then I looked up, and I was struck still, like stone, by the sight of a menacing four-legged creature: bulging eyes, a head of horns, dark long black hair touching the dirt, the scent of death. Silent. Staring directly at me.

Frozen, I noticed that the foul creature was bound to the fence by a red woven rope.

"I should be safe where I am" is what I probably thought to myself.

I carefully lifted my right foot to step backwards and flee, when suddenly, behind me, I heard the calm, familiar voice of an old man.

"Be not afraid."

There stood a tall man, towering over me with his wide shoulders, wearing a bright white shirt that seemed to radiate in the shadows of the corridor. His hair was all white, and so was the full moustache above his calm lips. I recognised him immediately. He was our neighbour Mr. Zorab, father of five sons; a man made of kindness.

"Be not afraid, go forward," he insisted in a warm, fatherly manner.

I looked forward again at the dark creature. He was still staring at me. But now he looked smaller than before, and farther away than I had first imagined. He was tied up in the middle of the corridor, and I was only one step into it.

With newfound confidence, I took a step forward, and then another. Each felt easier and longer than the one before. As I approached the creature known as a goat, I grew braver by the moment. Step by step, I reached the goat. With the next step I passed him, and now Mr. Goat wasn't scary anymore.

With no fear left to counter, I took big, proud steps. By the end of the corridor I held my head high, and with a victorious step I walked out into the open playing fields.

It was the day I conquered fear.
