Silly Snail

September 6, 2026

O' snail of the forest
Spirals of mist
Wise and weary
Eternally crawling
Did he also curse you?

In a silly forest there lived a silly-willy snail. His silly friends called him Billy, that silly-billy! He feasted on the forest's silly vegetation. His favorites were the silly shrooms. When he took a silly amount of the shrooms, he became even more of a silly Billy! He liked them so much that the forest residents called them silly-billy-shrooms.

Silly Billy is an addict.
