Bitter

November 19, 2026

Once
In my youth
In those days
Bitter cold
I laid by the side of a creek

Wind rose up
Gave me a kiss

Embraced

I shed a tear
For my forgotten self

Head in my hands

I shed a tear
For what I have become

Carrion birds
Circle above me
Neither friend nor foe

One came to have a bite
Beak to my flesh
It retched

My body has turned bitter

Older Be not afraid
