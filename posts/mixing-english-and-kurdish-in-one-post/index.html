Mixing English and Kurdish in one post

October 1, 2026 typography bidi

This paragraph is mostly English, but it mentions کوردی in the middle, and then carries on. The comma after the Kurdish word has to stay on the English side.

ئەم پەرەگرافە بە کوردی دەستپێدەکات، بەڵام ناوی Eleventy و ڕستەیەکی English inside it هەیە. پاشان بەردەوام دەبێت.

A sentence that ends with a Kurdish word: کتێب. The full stop belongs to the English sentence, not to the Kurdish word.

ساڵی 2024 بوو، و ژمارەی ١٢٣ بە ئەرەبی‑ھیندی نووسراوە.

Forcing a direction

Auto-detection reads the first strong character, so a Kurdish line that opens with a Latin brand name guesses wrong. Override it:

Eleventy چوارچێوەیەکی ستاتیکە کە بە JavaScript نووسراوە.

Without that attribute the same line would lay out left-to-right.
