A day with you

November 25, 2026

How would our days be?

Would you wake me up with little kisses?
Would I make you the breakfast you liked when you had it for brunch?
Would you let me hear your joy?
Would we share a table and split bread?
Would we go to the shop and forget the thing you wanted to get?
Would you read with me my favorite poem?

Would you make me pancakes?
Would we light candles?
Would we walk in the yard?
Would you light a fire by the creek?
Would I play the banjo for you?
Would we feel each other's warmth?

Would we make a nest?
Would we ruffle some feathers?
Would you get mud on me?
Would we bathe together?
Would we sing our songs?
Would we find where we belong?

Would you take me for a stroll?
Would we ditch and skip the toll?
Would we ignore the signs?
Would you rather fly instead?
Would the rain get us both wet?
Would we make it to the end?

Would you look into the abyss with me?
Would you hold my hands when I cry?
Would you make me milk and honey when I am cold?
Would we joke when we are old?

Would we carve pumpkins together?
Watch the leaves fall together?
Have the sun set on us
Together Together
Plant seeds
And blossom together?
