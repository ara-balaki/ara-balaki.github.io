# Fonts

Both faces are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 and were
fetched from Google Fonts by `scripts/install-fonts.py`.

- `latin.woff2`, `latin-italic.woff2` — Literata, variable 400–900 with
  optical sizes. https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Literata
- `arabic.woff2`, `arabic-bold.woff2` — Scheherazade New 400 / 700, by SIL.
  https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Scheherazade+New

These are the Latin and Arabic subsets only. Re-run the script to update.
